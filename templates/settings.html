Packing costs

Default costs taken off every sale that's posted. Set a different amount on any listing (Inventory → the item), or on a single sale. Car boot and in-person sales are skipped.

UK National Living Wage is £12.71 an hour (age 21+, from April 2026). 5 minutes a parcel at that rate is £1.06.

If you pack yourself, handling still comes off your profit so you can see what each sale would make with paid help, but it's left out of your tax figures: your own time isn't an allowable expense. Packaging always counts (box 18).
